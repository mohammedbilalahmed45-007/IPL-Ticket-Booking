About IPL 2026

The Indian Premier League (IPL) is a professional Twenty20 cricket league in India, established by the Board of Control for Cricket in India (BCCI) in 2008. It has become one of the most popular cricket leagues in the world, attracting top players from around the globe. The IPL features franchise teams representing different cities in India, and it is known for its high-energy matches, entertainment, and fan engagement.
